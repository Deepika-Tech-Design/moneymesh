ABOUT MONEYMESH

Built around the problem first — not the financial product.

Most finance apps expect users to already know whether they need budgeting, UPI support, a loan calculator, an insurance tool or a complaint portal. MoneyMesh starts with a simpler question: “What problem are you facing?”

The platform maps 50 common financial problems into 10 understandable themes and connects them to practical tools. The smart finder is intentionally browser-based, so basic problem discovery can work without sending the user's query to an external AI service.

MoneyMesh does not predict an exact bureau score. It explains common behaviours that can affect credit health.

Pay on time

Usually positive when consistent

Lower dependence is generally healthier

Can be a warning signal

24. Emergency Borrowing Guide

Urgency makes expensive borrowing look easier than it is. Check affordability and warning signs before committing.

Safer decision sequence

1. Reduce the amount

Ask whether part of the expense can be delayed, negotiated or paid in stages.

2. Use non-debt support first

Emergency savings, insurance claim, employer assistance or trusted family support may avoid high-cost debt.

3. Compare formal offers

Look at total repayment, fees, tenure and late-charge terms — not just “instant approval.”

4. Walk away from red flags

Avoid lenders demanding upfront “release” fees, OTP/PIN, remote access or unclear written terms.
